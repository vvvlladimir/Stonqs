Privacy

Stonqs has no account, no server and no telemetry. Your portfolio is a database on your own disk, encrypted if you give the profile a password. The author never receives any of it.

What the app connects to

These are all the network requests the app makes, and when.

WhatWhere it goesWhen
PricesYahoo Finance by default; Twelve Data, EODHD or Kraken if you configure themWhen quotes are refreshed
Exchange ratesEuropean Central Bank, Frankfurter, YahooWith quotes
Inflation figuresEurostat, IMFOnly if you pick a region for real returns
Instrument lookupYahoo, OpenFIGIWhen you search for or identify an instrument
AI assistantThe provider you configured, with your keyOnly when you use it
Update checkGitHub ReleasesOnce a day; can be turned off in Settings

Each of these services sees your IP address and what was asked (a ticker, a currency pair), under its own privacy policy. None of them receives your holdings, amounts or accounts, except the AI provider, and only for the questions you send it after you have allowed it to read your portfolio.

This website

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other servers. The details, in German as the law requires, are in the Datenschutzerklärung.
